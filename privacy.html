Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

This policy explains the main categories of information am-offer.net may collect and how that information may be used when people create accounts, post listings, make offers, send messages, save searches, or contact support.

1. Information You Provide

  • Account information such as name or display name, email address, password credentials, and optional phone number.
  • Listing information such as title, description, category, location, price, photos, and optional contact preferences.
  • Messages, offers, reports, support requests, community posts, and reflections you submit through the service.

2. Information Collected Automatically

The service may collect technical information such as device type, browser, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, pages viewed, feature usage, timestamps, and security logs.

3. How We Use Information

  • Operate accounts, listings, offers, messaging, favorites, saved searches, and alerts.
  • Show relevant local content and improve search and site functionality.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, security threats, and policy violations.
  • Respond to support requests and reports.
  • Maintain, analyze, and improve the service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect users, the service, and third parties.

4. Phone Number Privacy

A phone number is optional. It should not be displayed publicly on a listing unless the user expressly chooses to show it for that listing. Users may choose whether calls and/or text messages are acceptable where those options are available.

5. Messages and Offers

Private messages and offers are intended for the participants in the relevant conversation. am-offer.net may access or preserve them when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, user reports, troubleshooting, legal compliance, or service operation.

6. Sharing of Information

am-offer.net does not need to sell personal information in order to operate the marketplace. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the site, with authorities when legally required, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

7. Public Information

Listings, profile display names, selected contact information, community posts, and approved reflections may be visible publicly depending on the feature and the privacy choices you make.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The site may use cookies or similar storage technologies for sign-in, preferences, security, analytics, and essential site functionality.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce policies, prevent fraud, and comply with law. Retention periods may differ by data type.

10. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Choices

  • Update certain account information through account settings.
  • Choose whether to display a phone number on a listing.
  • Delete listings you no longer want published.
  • Contact support regarding account or privacy questions.

12. Children

am-offer.net is intended for users who are legally able to use the service and enter into the relevant transactions. The service is not designed as a child-directed platform.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes may be posted on the site with a revised effective date.

14. Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the Contact Us page.
