Account help

Creating an account

Enter your display name, email, and a password you choose. You can complete the rest of your profile information after you log in to your account.

Member approval

am-offer.net can operate in either Manual Approval or Automatic Approval mode. In Manual Approval, a new account remains pending until an administrator activates it. In Automatic Approval, the account becomes active immediately after registration.

Your password

  • Use at least 8 characters and choose a password that is difficult to guess.
  • Your existing password is never shown to an administrator.
  • Do not send your password to another member or to am-offer.net support.

Forgot your password?

Use Forgot password? on the login page. A secure reset link is sent to your email. The administrator does not retrieve or manually read your existing password.

Pending or inactive account

If your account is waiting for approval, the login page will tell you that the request is pending. If you believe an account was rejected or disabled by mistake, contact am-offer.net support.

Security: am-offer.net administrators can activate, suspend, or disable an account, but they cannot see your current password.
